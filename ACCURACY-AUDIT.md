# Accuracy Audit Report — grimdarkprimer.com

**Audited:** All 10 faction pages + painting.astro + getting-started.astro  
**Edition focus:** 10th edition (Warhammer 40,000 10th ed, launched June 2023)  
**Methodology:** Files read in full, Combat Patrol contents verified against Games Workshop product pages (via adeptusars.com, which mirrors GW box contents), lore cross-checked against established canon.

> **Summary note:** Every single faction page has wrong Combat Patrol box contents. This is the most widespread and critical category of error. Several pages also have outdated game mechanic descriptions that reference 8th/9th edition rules rather than 10th edition.

---

## Space Marines (`space-marines.astro`)

### ✅ Accurate
- "nineteen gene-seed organs" — correct lore (19 gene-seed implants)
- "Mk X Tacticus power armor" — correct for current era Space Marines
- "over a thousand Chapters" — correct (roughly 1,000+ Chapters)
- Blood Angels "terrifying flaw that drives them toward berserk fury" — correct (refers to the Black Rage / Red Thirst)
- Space Wolves "keep giant wolves as companions" — correct (Fenrisian Wolves)
- Dark Angels described as "betrayal ten thousand years ago" — correct (refers to the Fallen/Caliban during the Horus Heresy)
- Lore overview of the Great Crusade and Chapters is broadly accurate
- Paint names used: Macragge Blue (✓ real Base/Spray), Nuln Oil (✓), Calgar Blue (✓ real Layer paint), Retributor Armour (✓), Reikland Fleshshade (✓), Leadbelcher (✓), Ironbreaker (✓)

### ⚠️ Potential Issues

- **Claim:** "Includes a Captain in Terminator Armor, a squad of Infernus Marines, a Brutalis Dreadnought, and Outriders on bikes."
- **Issue:** **CRITICAL — WRONG box contents.** The Combat Patrol: Space Marines contains 5x Terminators, 5x Infernus Marines, 1x Captain in Terminator Armour, and 1x Librarian in Terminator Armour. There is no Brutalis Dreadnought and no Outriders in this box. Two units listed do not exist in the box; two units in the actual box are not mentioned.
- **Suggested fix:** "Includes a Captain in Terminator Armour, a Librarian in Terminator Armour, a squad of Terminators, and five Infernus Marines. It's a Terminator-focused force that showcases the Chapter's heavy infantry."

---

- **Claim:** "Each Chapter has its own unique rules (called Chapter Tactics)"
- **Issue:** "Chapter Tactics" is a 9th edition term. In 10th edition, Chapter-specific rules are delivered through **Detachment rules** and each army's **Army Rule**. The term "Chapter Tactics" was retired with the 9th edition codex.
- **Suggested fix:** Change "Chapter Tactics" to "Detachment rules" or "Chapter abilities."

### ✅ Accurate

- "Toughness 5" for Ork Boys — correct in 10th edition
- Paint names: Warboss Green (✓ real Layer paint), Ork Flesh (✓ real Contrast paint)

### ⚠️ Potential Issues

- **Claim:** "Includes a Beastboss (your warlord), Ork Boyz (the core troop), Deffkoptas (fast attack bikes)..." and cost breakdown note reads "Warboss, Boyz, Deffkoptas, Squig Herd"
- **Issue:** **CRITICAL — WRONG box contents.** The Combat Patrol: Orks contains: 1x Beastboss, 4x Squighog Boyz + 1x Bomb Squig, and 20x Beast Snagga Boyz. There are **no Deffkoptas** in this box. The note also mistakenly calls the Beastboss a "Warboss" (different unit), and lists a "Squig Herd" which doesn't exist in this box.
- **Suggested fix:** "Includes a Beastboss (your warlord), 20 Beast Snagga Boyz (your core beast-hunting troops), and 4 Squighog Boyz with a Bomb Squig (elite cavalry riding giant squigs). A Beast Snagga-themed force that showcases the horde + cavalry dynamic."

---

- **Claim:** "Toughness 5 means a lot of weapons that would kill a Space Marine need two hits to kill an Ork Boy."
- **Issue:** Misleading phrasing. Higher Toughness doesn't mean weapons require two hits — it means wound rolls are harder. A weapon that wounds a Space Marine (T4) on 4+ wounds an Ork Boy (T5) on 5+. Ork Boys also have 1 Wound each. The "two hits" framing is mechanically inaccurate.
- **Suggested fix:** "Toughness 5 means many weapons that wound a Space Marine on 4+ only wound an Ork Boy on 5+ — making the whole mob more resilient to small arms fire."

- **Claim:** "Includes a Winged Tyranid Prime (fast, hard-hitting leader), Neurogaunts (cheap troops), Barbgaunts (mid-range shooting), and a Psychophage (big monstrous creature)."
- **Issue:** **CRITICAL — WRONG box contents.** The Combat Patrol: Tyranids contains: 1x Winged Tyranid Prime, 1x Psychophage, 3x Von Ryan's Leapers, 5x Barbgaunts, 20x **Termagants** (not Neurogaunts), and 2x Ripper Swarms. **Neurogaunts** are a separate, newer unit (introduced in the Leviathan 10th edition starter box) with completely different rules from Termagants. - **Claim:** "Hive Fleet Behemoth... the first Hive Fleet to reach the galaxy — and the one that destroyed an entire Space Marine Chapter."
- **Issue:** **UNCERTAIN — likely inaccurate.** Hive Fleet Behemoth was the first recognized Hive Fleet to threaten the Imperium at scale, but it devastated the Ultramarines (particularly their 1st Company at the Battle for Macragge) without destroying the Chapter entirely. No Space Marine Chapter was completely wiped out by Hive Fleet Behemoth specifically. This appears to conflate Behemoth's damage with possibly Hive Fleet Kraken's destruction of the Lamenters or other Chapter-level casualties. Needs verification.

---

- **Claim:** "Termagants (24-model box)"

- **Suggested fix:** "Termagants (20-model box)"

---

- **Claim:** `{ name: 'Casandora Yellow (Contrast)', use: 'Talons and claws', required: true }`
- **Issue:** **Casandora Yellow is a Shade paint (wash), NOT a Contrast paint.** It is listed in the Citadel range as a Shade. The correct Contrast paint for yellow-orange tones would be something like **Iyanden Yellow** (Contrast). Casandora Yellow used over Wraithbone would work as a yellow wash but calling it a "Contrast" paint is factually wrong.
- **Suggested fix:** Change to `{ name: 'Casandora Yellow (Shade)', use: 'Wash on talons and claws for yellow tint', required: true }` — or replace with `Iyanden Yellow (Contrast)` if a true Contrast paint is desired.

- **Claim:** "Includes an Ethereal (commander), Fire Warriors (core infantry), Pathfinders (your Markerlight unit — essential to how the army works), and a Broadside Battlesuit (heavy support)."
- **Issue:** **CRITICAL — WRONG box contents.** The Combat Patrol: T'au Empire contains: 1x **Commander in Enforcer Battlesuit** (or Coldstar — not an Ethereal), 1x **Devilfish** (transport — not a Broadside), 10x Fire Warriors (Strike or Breacher), 10x Pathfinders, and 7x Drone models. - **Claim:** "Pathfinders and drones illuminate targets, **stacking benefits** that make the rest of your army shoot better at that target — **improved accuracy, more shots, ignoring cover**."
- **Issue:** **OUTDATED — describes 8th/9th edition Markerlights.** In 10th edition, the Markerlight system was significantly redesigned and simplified. The old stacking system (where each Markerlight token added a different benefit: improved BS, more shots, ignoring cover, etc.) was removed. In 10th edition, Markerlights give a single, non-stacking bonus to friendly units shooting at marked targets. The multi-benefit stacking description is incorrect for 10th edition.
- **Suggested fix:** Update to describe the 10th edition Markerlight mechanic specifically, which provides a +1 to hit rolls (verify exact wording against the current T'au codex).
